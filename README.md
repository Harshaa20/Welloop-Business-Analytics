# Welloop Business Analytics: Product Strategy for a Token-Based Sports Equipment Exchange Platform

## Project Overview

This project presents a business analytics solution developed from my master's thesis research in collaboration with Welloop AB, a Swedish sports technology company focused on improving youth sports participation through digital innovation.

The objective was to evaluate the business feasibility of introducing a token-based sports equipment exchange platform that enables young athletes to exchange used sports equipment within their sports communities.

Using qualitative interview data collected from athletes and coaches across multiple youth sports teams, this project transforms research findings into actionable business intelligence using SQL, Python, and Tableau.

---

## Business Problem

The rising cost of sports equipment creates a financial barrier that contributes to reduced participation and increased dropout among young athletes.

Although many athletes own reusable equipment, there is currently no structured and trusted platform that facilitates equipment exchange within local sports communities.

From a business perspective, Welloop needed evidence to answer several strategic questions:

- Is there sufficient user demand for an equipment exchange platform?
- Which equipment categories should be prioritised during MVP development?
- Can existing supply satisfy expected demand?
- What risks could prevent successful platform adoption?
- How should the platform be designed to maximise adoption?

This project provides data-driven answers to each of these questions.

---

## Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Evaluate athlete willingness to adopt a token-based equipment exchange platform.
- Measure the perceived impact on reducing sports dropout.
- Assess coach perspectives on implementation feasibility.
- Identify the highest-demand equipment categories.
- Compare expected demand with potential equipment supply.
- Understand preferred exchange methods.
- Identify the major product adoption risks.
- Support strategic product and MVP decision-making.

---

## Dataset

The analysis is based on qualitative interview data collected during my master's thesis research.

The dataset contains responses from youth athletes and coaches across football and basketball clubs in Sweden.

Data preparation included qualitative coding, categorisation, aggregation, and business-focused analysis before visualisation.

## Key Business Insights

### Athlete Adoption

- Nearly 90% of athletes expressed positive willingness to adopt the platform.

### Sports Dropout

- More than 80% of participants believed the platform could help reduce sports dropout by improving equipment accessibility.

### Coach Support

- Coaches generally supported implementation, provided operational guidelines and governance mechanisms were established.

### Product Priorities

The most requested equipment categories were:

- Shoes
- Jerseys
- Shin Pads

These should be prioritised during MVP development.

### Supply Analysis

Several equipment categories demonstrated supply shortages compared to expected demand, highlighting opportunities for donation campaigns and inventory planning.

### Product Risks

Trust emerged as the dominant concern among participants, followed by fairness and user participation.

The findings indicate that user verification, transparent exchange mechanisms, and trust-building features should be prioritised.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategic recommendations are proposed:

- Proceed with MVP development.
- Prioritise high-demand equipment categories.
- Introduce user verification mechanisms.
- Promote community donation initiatives.
- Design the platform around a mobile-first exchange experience.
- Establish governance policies to ensure fair equipment exchanges.
